Privacy Policy

Creative Management Solutions

Last updated: August 2026

This page provides general information about privacy practices for Creative Management Solutions and its digital products. Product-specific privacy policies may apply where a product collects or processes information.

Information We May Process

Depending on the product and features used, information may include account information, device information, usage information, transaction information, location information when a location feature is used, and information voluntarily provided by users.

How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to provide product functionality, maintain security, improve products, provide support, process transactions, comply with applicable law, and communicate important service information.

Data Storage

Some CMS products are designed with local/offline storage. For example, VibeFit's core experience is designed to operate offline with user data stored locally. Optional online services may introduce additional processing as described in the relevant product policy.

Third Parties

Where third-party services are used, their processing may be governed by their own privacy policies. Product-specific disclosures should be reviewed before launch.

Data Retention and Deletion

Retention and deletion practices vary by product and feature. Users may contact us for product-specific privacy or deletion requests.
