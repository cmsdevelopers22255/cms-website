Live Build · Play Store Preparation Wellness Technology

VibeFit

An offline-first fitness and wellness companion bringing workouts, yoga, nutrition, progress tracking, lifestyle planning and wellness guidance into one focused experience.

Workout Tracking Yoga & Meditation Indian Nutrition Offline-First Local Data Progress Analytics Lifestyle Planner Smart Reminders Privacy Focused

What it brings together

  • Workout programs, exercise tracking and workout history
  • Yoga, meditation, mobility and recovery experiences
  • Indian-friendly nutrition and meal tracking
  • Weight, body metrics, photos and progress analytics
  • Calendar, lifestyle planning, reminders and reports

Product philosophy

VibeFit is designed around an offline-first experience so essential fitness tracking remains available without continuous internet access. User data can be stored locally for a fast, private and dependable experience.

Product Development Local Commerce Technology

KokanKart

A hyperlocal commerce platform connecting customers with trusted local vendors and regional products, with customer, vendor, delivery and administration journeys connected in one ecosystem.

Hyperlocal Marketplace Local Vendors Regional Products Smart Cart Order Tracking Vendor Dashboard Delivery Flow Admin Operations Area-Based Commerce

Connected ecosystem

  • Customers discover products available in their area
  • Local vendors manage products, stock, prices and orders
  • Delivery partners can manage assigned deliveries and status
  • Admin operations support vendor, product and order management
  • Area-based commerce supports a focused local launch strategy

Product direction

KokanKart is designed to make local shopping easier while helping regional vendors participate in digital commerce. The long-term model can evolve from focused local markets into a wider network.

Our Approach

From an idea to a product people can use.

CMS keeps the process deliberately focused: understand the problem, build the right foundation, ship a useful product, then improve it through real-world learning.

01

Identify

Find a real user or business problem worth solving and understand the context around it.

02

Architect

Define product scope, experience, technology and a foundation that can evolve with the product.

03

Build

Turn the product vision into a reliable, focused experience with thoughtful engineering and design.

04

Launch

Put the product in real users’ hands, observe actual behaviour and learn from the market.

05

Iterate

Use feedback, data and experience to improve quality, usefulness and long-term product value.
