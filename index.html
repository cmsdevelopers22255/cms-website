01 / About CMS

We don't build software for others. We build products we believe in.

CMS is a product-focused technology company creating, launching, and operating its own digital platforms.

Creative Management Solutions (CMS Group) is an India-focused digital product studio focused on building independent technology products that solve practical, everyday problems.

Our current product ecosystem spans fitness and wellness, hyperlocal commerce, gym management, and retail business operations.

CMS follows a simple philosophy: identify a real problem, engineer a useful product, launch it, learn from users, and continuously improve.

03 / Engineering Approach

From real-world problem to continuously evolving product.

CMS follows a structured product engineering pipeline designed around ownership, reliability, speed, and continuous improvement.

01

Identify

Understand a genuine problem and validate the opportunity before building.

02

Architecture

Design scalable foundations around the product's actual requirements.

03

Local-First Engine

Prioritize reliability, performance, privacy, and resilient user experiences where appropriate.

04

Deploy

Ship usable products, observe real-world usage, and make the product available to users.

05

Iterate

Learn from customers, improve continuously, and scale what works.
